### Data

- Simulated: U standard normal (unmeasured); habit x1 = −0.8 U + 0.6 e; treatment x2 = 0.6 U + 0.8 e; outcome latent 1.2 U + 0.5 x2 + logistic noise, outcome = latent > 0; test result x3 = latent + 0.8 e; x4 pure noise. 20,000 training rows (seed 0), 20,000 fresh rows (seed 1), interventions simulated on 200,000 rows (seed 2).
- Machinery: `standarderror/ml/decide.py`, tested in `tests/test_ml.py`; scikit-learn's HistGradientBoostingClassifier, LogisticRegression and permutation_importance (AUC, 5 repeats).
- Where this stops: Pearl, *Causality* (2009); Hernán and Robins, *Causal Inference: What If* (2020); Zhao and Hastie, "Causal interpretations of black-box models", *Journal of Business & Economic Statistics* (2021); episodes 16 to 18 of this series.

### Reproducibility

- **environment**: standarderror=0.1.0, python=3.13.16, numpy=2.4.4
- **code blocks**: executed at build time; the values the prose quotes are pinned, so drift fails the build
- **determinism**: seeds 0, 1 and 2; seconds of CPU
