### Data

- Simulated: each subject has a signature in ten features (normal, sd 2) added to every one of its rows, and a propensity (normal, sd 2) added to every row's log-odds; the part of the outcome that carries over to new subjects is 1.5 x_1 + x_2. Six data sets per design (seeds 0 to 5); new subjects: 2,000 of them with 5 rows each (seeds 1,000 to 1,005).
- Machinery: `standarderror/ml/rows.py`, tested in `tests/test_ml.py`; scikit-learn's LogisticRegression, KNeighborsClassifier (k = 5), RandomForestClassifier (50 trees), KFold with shuffling and GroupKFold, five folds each.
- Where this stops: Kish, *Survey Sampling* (1965) on the design effect; Saeb et al., "The need to approximate the use-case in clinical machine learning", *GigaScience* (2017); Roberts et al., *Ecography* (2017); episode 3 of this series.

### Reproducibility

- **environment**: standarderror=0.1.0, python=3.13.16, numpy=2.4.4
- **code blocks**: executed at build time; the values the prose quotes are pinned, so drift fails the build
- **determinism**: seeds as listed; shuffled folds from the data set's seed; about two minutes of CPU
