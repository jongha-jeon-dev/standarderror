### Data

- Each claim's data are those of its episode: breast cancer, the digits and the diabetes data via scikit-learn, and the episodes' simulations; seed 0 reproduces the published value.
- Machinery: `standarderror/ml/course.py` (`CLAIMS`, `rerun_claims`), calling the functions in `leakage.py`, `imbalance.py`, `trees.py`, `reading.py`, `around.py`, `rows.py`, `decide.py` and `unsup.py`; tested in `tests/test_ml.py`.
- Where this stops: Bouthillier, Laurent and Vincent, "Unreproducible research is reproducible", *ICML* (2019); Gelman and Loken, "The garden of forking paths" (2013); episodes 1, 20 and 51 of this series.

### Reproducibility

- **environment**: standarderror=0.1.0, python=3.13.16, numpy=2.4.4
- **code blocks**: executed at build time; the values the prose quotes are pinned, so drift fails the build
- **determinism**: seeded throughout; about six minutes of CPU
