### Data

- Breast Cancer Wisconsin (Diagnostic), CC BY 4.0, via scikit-learn: 569 tumours, malignant as the positive class, one stratified 70/30 split (seed 0).
- Models: standardised logistic regression; histogram gradient boosting (200 rounds); the same boosting with Platt scaling (5-fold); an RBF SVM tuned over episode 35's 64 settings, with nested 5-fold CV. Comparison by 10-fold CV with the Nadeau and Bengio correction; McNemar's exact test on the held-out disagreements; 50 bootstrap refits for stability; two-sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov tests at 0.05/30 for the shift check.
- Machinery: `standarderror/ml/course.py` and `standarderror/ml/decide.py`, tested in `tests/test_ml.py`.
- Where this stops: Nadeau and Bengio, "Inference for the generalization error", *Machine Learning* (2003); Elkan, "The foundations of cost-sensitive learning", *IJCAI* (2001); episodes 1, 11, 14, 20, 29, 34, 35, 44 to 47, 61 and 63 of this series.

### Reproducibility

- **environment**: standarderror=0.1.0, python=3.13.16, numpy=2.4.4
- **code blocks**: executed at build time; the values the prose quotes are pinned, so drift fails the build
- **determinism**: seeded throughout; about fifteen seconds of CPU
