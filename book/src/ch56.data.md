### Data

- Simulated: Gaussian clusters in ten dimensions (seed 0); two of 500 points with standard deviations 1 and 10, and three of 300 with unit spread and centres 0, 6 and 66 along one axis.
- UCI optical digits (1,797 8x8 images, CC BY 4.0) via scikit-learn.
- Machinery: scikit-learn's TSNE (Barnes-Hut, PCA initialisation for the simulations; for the digits, random and then PCA initialisation, seeds 0-4 each) and HDBSCAN (minimum cluster size 25); `standarderror/ml/unsup.py`, tested in `tests/test_ml.py`.
- Where this stops: van der Maaten and Hinton, "Visualizing data using t-SNE", *JMLR* (2008); Wattenberg, Viégas and Johnson, "How to use t-SNE effectively", *Distill* (2016); Kobak and Berens, "The art of using t-SNE for single-cell transcriptomics", *Nature Communications* (2019); episodes 31 and 32 of this series.

### Reproducibility

- **environment**: standarderror=0.1.0, python=3.13.16, numpy=2.4.4
- **code blocks**: executed at build time; the values the prose quotes are pinned, so drift fails the build
- **determinism**: seeded throughout; about two minutes of CPU
